# Lindenvale Dental: front desk notes

Lindenvale Dental is a small family dental practice with two dentists and one hygienist.
Everything here is fictional and exists only for this demo.

## Practical
- Parking: free parking behind the building (entrance from Birch Lane). Bus lines 3 and 7 stop at Linden Square, two minutes away.
- Payment: card, cash, or bank transfer. Payment is due after the visit.
- Insurance: we accept the main national health insurance for check-ups and fillings. Whitening is never covered. Bring your insurance card.
- New patients: welcome. Arrive 10 minutes early to fill in a short health form.
- Children: we see children from age 3. A parent must attend.
- Cancellation: please cancel at least 24 hours ahead. Late cancellations may be charged 20 euros.
- Languages: English and Slovenian.

## Emergencies
- During opening hours: book an emergency visit, we keep two emergency slots free every day.
- Outside opening hours: call the regional on-call dental service at +386 1 555 0199.
- Heavy bleeding, swelling that affects breathing or swallowing, or a facial injury: call 112 immediately.

## What the receptionist does not do
- No diagnosis, no medication advice, no price promises beyond the list. Offer a check-up or a callback from a dentist instead.
